The project

A contemporary showroom where the product is the hero.

Interior design and execution for the Vismay showroom in Chennai, with a contemporary retail layout, custom display systems, premium finishes and carefully planned lighting.

Design highlights

What we built

A sculptural display

A curved brass-finish screen frames a round marble plinth, giving the key looks a stage of their own on the showroom floor.

Custom wall systems

Brass-finish shelving above hanging rails keeps the full range visible and easy to browse, from folded stock to hanging garments.

Lighting that sells

Recessed ceiling lights and backlit signage give an even, bright light that shows true colours and makes the feature walls stand out.
