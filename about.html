About the studio

Design, function andquality execution

Our story

A studio that designs your space and then builds it, so nothing is lost between the drawing and the site.

Stellarix Design Studio brings interior design, 3D visualisation, custom joinery, ceilings, lighting, finishes and site execution together under one roof. Each project has one team responsible for it from the first concept to the handover.

We are based in Salem, Tamil Nadu. We have completed showrooms in Hyderabad and Chennai, and a new showroom is under way in Madurai.

Our approach

What you can expect from us

01

Brand first

We start with your brand, your customers and your products. The design follows from them, not from a template.

02

Function before decoration

Circulation, storage, display and lighting are planned first, so the space works every day and not just on opening day.

03

Quality execution

Careful detailing, the right materials and close supervision on site, because a good design needs to be built well.

04

One team, one contact

Design, trades and site coordination are handled by one team, with a single point of contact for you.
