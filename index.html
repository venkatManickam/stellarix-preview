Our focus

Retail interiors that sell

A showroom is a sales tool. We plan every one around the customer's path through the store: what they see from the entrance, where they stop and how the product is lit and displayed.

  • Layouts that guide customers through the full range
  • Custom display systems for your products
  • Lighting planned around the merchandise and the brand
  • Finishes chosen for looks, durability and easy upkeep

We also design commercial and residential interiors.

How we work

A clear process, start to finish

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit the site, understand your brand, needs and budget, and take measurements.

  2. 02

    Concept & 3D

    Layouts, a material direction and 3D views, so you can see the space before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design development

    Detailed drawings, finishes, lighting and display details, with a clear scope and estimate.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Our team manages the site, the trades and the materials, with regular updates to you.

  5. 05

    Handover

    A final walkthrough and snag check, then the keys to a finished, ready-to-use space.
