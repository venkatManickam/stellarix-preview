Materials

Materials. Quality.Craftsmanship.

Chosen with purpose

At Stellarix Design Studio, every detail is chosen with purpose.

We use carefully selected, high-quality materials and trusted products to create interiors that combine elegance, durability, functionality and timeless design.

What we build with

Premium materials we work with

Plywood & Boards

Premium BWP, waterproof plywood, commercial plywood, MDF and HDHMR boards, each selected according to the specific application and performance requirements.

Laminates & Surface Finishes

A curated range of premium laminates, textures, colours and finishes to create refined and contemporary interiors.

Natural Veneers

Elegant natural veneer options for a sophisticated wood finish and a warm, timeless appearance.

Premium Hardware & Fittings

Quality hinges, drawer channels, handles, lift-up systems and accessories, including soft-close solutions for smoother everyday use.

Gypsum & Ceiling Systems

Quality gypsum boards and framing systems designed for clean, precise and durable ceiling finishes.

Paint & Finishing Systems

Premium interior paints and finishing materials, combined with proper surface preparation, for a smooth and lasting finish.

Glass & Aluminium

Quality glass and aluminium solutions for partitions, doors, cabinets and customised interior applications.

Adhesives & Sealants

Application-specific adhesives, sealants and fixing systems, selected for strength, durability and reliable performance.
