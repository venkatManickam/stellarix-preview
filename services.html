Services

Everything your space needs,under one roof

Take one service or the whole journey. Most clients choose design and execution together, so one team is responsible for the finished space.

01

Design

Interior Design & Planning

Space planning, layouts, circulation and zoning, with a material and colour direction that suits the brand and the budget.

3D Visualization & Concept Development

Mood boards, concepts and 3D views of the key areas, so every decision is made before work starts on site.

02

Spaces

Retail & Showroom Interiors

Fashion and retail showrooms planned around the customer's path, product display and lighting, with space for trial rooms, billing counters and back-of-house storage.

Commercial Interiors

Offices and commercial spaces that are practical to work in, easy to maintain and right for your brand.

Residential Interiors

Homes designed around the way your family lives, with storage, lighting and finishes planned room by room.

03

Build

Custom Furniture & Display Units

Display systems, shelving, counters and furniture made to measure for your products and your space.

False Ceiling & Lighting

Ceiling design with ambient, accent and display lighting, planned together so the product and the space both look their best.

Flooring & Wall Finishes

Flooring, wall panelling, paint and feature finishes chosen for appearance, durability and upkeep.

Electrical & MEP Coordination

Electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning points coordinated with the design, so services sit where the layout needs them.

04

Deliver

Complete Interior Execution

Turnkey execution of the approved design, with all trades, materials and finishing handled by our team.

Project Management & Site Coordination

Scheduling, supervision, quality checks and regular progress updates, through to the handover.

How we work

Our process

  1. 01

    Consultation & site visit

    We meet you, visit the site, take measurements and understand your brand, requirements, timeline and budget.

  2. 02

    Concept & 3D visualisation

    We present layouts, a material and lighting direction and 3D views of the key areas for your feedback.

  3. 03

    Design development & estimate

    Once the concept is approved, we prepare detailed drawings, finishes and specifications, with the scope and an estimate.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Our team coordinates the trades, materials and site work, with regular progress updates to you.

  5. 05

    Handover

    A final walkthrough, snag fixes and the handover of a finished space, ready for opening day.

Questions

Frequently asked

Have a question that is not here? Ask us.

Do you handle both design and execution?

Yes. We can take on the design only, or the design and complete execution. Most clients choose both, so that one team is responsible from the concept to the finished space.

Do you take projects outside Salem?

Yes. We are based in Salem and have completed showrooms in Hyderabad and Chennai, with a new showroom under way in Madurai.

Will I see the design before work starts?

Yes. We share layouts and 3D views of the key areas, and work starts on site only after you approve the design.

How long does a showroom interior take?

It depends on the size of the space and the scope of work. We agree a schedule with you before execution begins, and keep you updated throughout.

How is the cost worked out?

The cost depends on the approved design, the materials and the scope. We prepare an estimate after design development, so you know the cost before execution starts.

Can you work with our brand guidelines?

Yes. Retail interiors start from your brand: its colours, materials, signage and the way your products should be seen.
